# Venue FAQ — Global Azure Bootcamp 2026

## Registration & Check-in

**Q: Where do I pick up my badge?**
A: Registration is in the main lobby (Ground Floor). Doors open at 8:00 AM. Bring the confirmation
email on your phone or printed. Badges are sorted alphabetically by last name.

**Q: Can I register on the day?**
A: Yes, walk-in registration is available at the help desk for NZD $20. Card payment only.

**Q: I registered but didn't receive a confirmation email.**
A: Check your spam folder first. If not there, visit the help desk — they can look you up by name.

**Q: Is there a dedicated presenter Wi-Fi network?**
A: Yes, speakers get a separate high-bandwidth network. Credentials are in your speaker briefing pack.

**Q: Where can I find power outlets?**
A: Power strips are available along the walls in all session rooms and in the hallway seating areas.
The lounge on Level 2 has a full charging station with USB-A, USB-C, and power outlets.

## Food & Drink

**Q: Is lunch included?**
A: Yes! Lunch is included for all registered attendees. It's served in the Level 2 Atrium from
12:00–13:00. Show your badge to the catering staff. Menu includes hot and cold options, vegan and
gluten-free clearly labelled.

**Q: Are there coffee/tea options?**
A: Sponsored coffee stations are on every floor, open all day. Free for all attendees.

**Q: Are there options for dietary restrictions?**
A: Yes. Vegan, gluten-free, halal, and nut-free options are available at lunch. For severe allergies,
speak to the catering team at the service counter directly.

---

## After-Party

**Q: Is there an after-party?**
A: Yes! The official after-party is at the **Britomart rooftop bar**, 5-minute walk from the venue.
It starts at **6:30 PM** and runs until 9:00 PM. Entry is free for badge holders.
Drinks vouchers (2x standard drink) are included with your badge.

**Q: Is the after-party 18+?**
A: Yes, the venue is licensed. You must show photo ID at the door.

---

## Venue & Logistics

**Q: Is there a cloakroom?**
A: Yes, free cloakroom service in the main lobby. Opens at 8:00 AM, closes 30 minutes after
the last session ends.

**Q: Where is parking?**
A: Wilson Parking on Mayoral Drive is the closest. Show your badge at the help desk for a
discounted parking voucher (up to 10 hours).

**Q: Is the venue accessible?**
A: Fully wheelchair accessible. Automatic doors on Federal Street. Lifts to all floors.
Accessible restrooms on every level. Live captioning in Rooms A and B.

**Q: Where is the quiet room?**
A: Room 3F on Level 3. No presentations — a calm space to decompress, recharge devices, or take
a private call. Opens 8:30 AM.

---

## Speakers & Sessions

**Q: Where do speakers check in?**
A: Speaker check-in is at the green desk near Room A (Level 2). Please arrive 30 minutes before
your session for AV testing.

**Q: Will sessions be recorded?**
A: Most sessions in Room A and Room B will be recorded. Recordings will be available on the
Global Azure Bootcamp YouTube channel within 2 weeks.

**Q: Is there a speaker dinner the night before?**
A: Yes — Friday evening at Pasta & Theory restaurant, 7:00 PM. Invite-only for confirmed speakers.
Contact the organising team if you haven't received your invite.
